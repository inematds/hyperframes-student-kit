Three Core Categories to Master
01
Strategy
Define the north star before writing a single line of code. Clarity on intent is the difference between shipping fast and shipping right.
02
Execution
Ruthless focus on the most important task each week. Remove blockers before they compound. This is where strategy becomes reality.
03
Leverage
Multiply your output with the right tools, people, and systems. One well-placed lever beats ten times the effort.
