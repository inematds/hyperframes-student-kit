Three Key Takeaways
1
Clarity beats speed every time
Teams that slow down to align save weeks of rework downstream. Shared context is the real force multiplier.
2
Leverage compounds faster than effort
One strategic system or automation can outperform months of manual work. Invest in leverage early and the returns stack.
3
Ship, learn, repeat — not plan, plan, plan
Real feedback is worth more than any prediction. The goal is to generate data, not to be right on your first attempt.
